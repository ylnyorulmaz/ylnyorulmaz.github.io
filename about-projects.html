Building as a continuing practice

Independent work gives me room to take an idea through product definition, implementation and release. It brings technical decisions, interaction design and product judgment into the same process.

The portfolio spans games, practical utilities, reflection tools and data analysis. Some are live products; others are ongoing work. The distinction matters: each entry below retains its current portfolio status, and ongoing projects are presented as work in progress.
